Business Question

Which categories drive the most revenue — and which have the worst customer experience? Where is product quality investment most urgently needed?

Discount Lift — The Most Important Metric in M6

Discount Lift = (AOV with discount − AOV without discount) / AOV without discount × 100

If lift is 49% — discounted orders have 49% higher basket value. Discounts are attracting genuinely bigger buyers, not just reducing margin on existing orders. This is strategic discounting.

If lift is near 0% — discounts are not growing baskets. The same customers are buying the same amount and just paying less. This is margin destruction.

Action: Cut discounts first in categories with low lift. Protect discounts in categories with high lift.

Key Findings

Fruits & Vegetables has the highest refund rate — Perishables are the hardest to guarantee quality on delivery. This category typically sits in the High Revenue / High Risk quadrant — the most urgent quality investment target.

Personal Care drives the strongest retention — Customers who buy Personal Care tend to have higher satisfaction and lower at-risk rates. Expanding this category is the lowest-risk growth lever.

Discounts lift basket size most in Grocery — Grocery and Dairy show the highest AOV lift from discounts (~40–49%), meaning discount spend in these categories actually drives bigger orders. Beverages and Snacks show the lowest lift — discounts there mostly give away margin
