Business Question

What drives customers to stop ordering — and which companies, categories, and experience types are most at risk?


Key Findings

Fruits & Vegetables has the highest refund rate — Perishables are harder to guarantee on quality at delivery. This is the category most in need of cold-chain investment.

Both refund AND delay is the breaking point — Customers who experienced both have the lowest order frequency and lowest ratings of any segment. This is the at-risk bucket that needs immediate intervention.
