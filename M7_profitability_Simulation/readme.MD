Break-Even AOV Formula

                  (1 − ops_cost_pct − discount_depth × discount_rate)

A company whose current AOV is far above its break-even AOV has a larger margin buffer. Swiggy Instamart's higher AOV (₹646) gives it the widest buffer — its revenue quality makes it the most financially resilient despite having the slowest delivery.


Key Findings

Growing AOV beats cutting discounts. Scenario S2 (+15% AOV) produces better margin improvement than S1 (cut discounts 10%). Reason: discounted orders have ~49% higher AOV than full-price orders. Removing discounts eliminates high-value customers alongside the cost, partially cancelling the benefit.

Discounts are attracting your biggest spenders. This is the counterintuitive finding that makes M7 stand out. The instinct is to cut discounts to save margin. The data says that move has a partial self-defeating mechanism.

Combined scenario (S4) is the best strategy. Growing AOV 15% while cutting delivery cost ₹5/km consistently produces the highest margin across all three companies.

Swiggy has the most margin buffer. Despite being slowest, Swiggy's ₹646 AOV means the widest gap above break-even. Revenue quality matters more than speed for financial resilience.
