Business Question

What actually causes delivery delays — and which companies, cities, and partners underperform on speed?

Key Findings

Distance explains only ~25% of delivery time. The Pearson R² (Python Cell 2) shows ~0.25, meaning 75% of delivery time variance comes from store prep time, traffic, and partner skill — not distance. This is the root cause finding: route optimisation helps at the margin, but prep time improvement has far more impact.

Zepto's efficiency is structural. Its efficiency ratio advantage holds across all distance buckets, meaning it's not just because Zepto operates in closer proximity to customers. Something in Zepto's process — likely prep time — is fundamentally faster.

Partner rating is a real signal. Elite tier partners (4.5+ rating) deliver measurably faster than Poor tier (<3.0), validating partner quality as a genuine operational lever, not just a perception metric.

Haridwar is Swiggy's biggest operational outlier. SQL Query 6 surfaces ~17.7% slow orders vs near-zero in other cities. The Python z-score method (Cell 3) confirms this is statistically significant (z > 2.0)
